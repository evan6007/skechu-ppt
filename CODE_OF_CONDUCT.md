# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project. Be constructive, assume good intent, discuss ideas rather than people, and respect privacy and intellectual property.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, publishing private information, or sustained disruption. Maintainers may edit, reject, lock, or remove contributions that violate these expectations.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the security contact described in `SECURITY.md`.
